Take slash commands. A command's Request URL is `layerInteractivity`'s path, and `SlackChat.interaction` takes a command's fields, from the form or a `slash_commands` envelope. `normalizeInteraction` now gives `CommandInvoked` for them, where it gave none. `respond` is answered in the response body with `response_type`, `defer` with an empty 200, a modal through the command's `trigger_id`, and a late command with `Late`. `Chat.followUp` posts once to the command's `response_url`, which must be on a Slack host, for 30 minutes. The descriptor lists `commands` and `ephemeral`. A modal a command opened submits without a `messageId`. The app needs the `commands` scope. A spent or expired `response_url` answers 404 `used_url` or `expired_url`, and `followUp` fails `NotFound` with detail `interaction_expired` for both. A mention in a command's answer goes out escaped and notifies nobody. `edit` on a private answer's `messageId` fails `NotFound`, as `chat.update` answers `message_not_found` for it.
