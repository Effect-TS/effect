Add the experimental `effect/encoding/Protobuf` module for Protocol Buffers binary encoding and decoding. `Protobuf.toCodec` derives a codec from a schema annotated for protobuf, such as one generated by `protoc-gen-effect`: `Protobuf.Message`s of fields numbered with `Protobuf.field`, `Protobuf.Oneof`, `Protobuf.Enum`, `Protobuf.OpenEnum`, `Protobuf.Map`, extensions, `Any`, unknown fields, and the well-known types.

Add the experimental `effect/encoding/ProtobufDescriptor` and `effect/encoding/ProtobufPlugin` modules with schemas for `descriptor.proto` (`FileDescriptorSet`, `FileDescriptorProto`, …) and `plugin.proto` (`CodeGeneratorRequest`, `CodeGeneratorResponse`). Fields a message does not declare, such as custom options, are kept and written back.
