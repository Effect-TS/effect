Add `@effect/protobuf-generator`, whose `protoc-gen-effect` command generates Effect Schema modules from `.proto` files, as a plugin for buf and protoc or with `protoc-gen-effect generate` from a descriptor set: messages, enums, oneofs, maps, groups, extensions, recursive messages, defaults, well-known types, and a registry per file, for proto2, proto3, and editions 2023 and 2024.
