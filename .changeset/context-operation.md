Add `Context.Operation`, `Context.Operation.value` and `Context.Operations` (`@stability unstable`): a service member defined as its own key, so a program bundles only the implementations it calls and a test replaces one of them, and a record service over such keys that `yield*`, `Layer.succeed`, `Effect.provideService` and the other `Context`, `Layer` and `Effect` functions accept unchanged. A record service is its members: providing it writes one slot per member, reading it returns a plain object of the members (not the provided object), and its members are properties of the key. `Stream.Operation` and `Sink.Operation` define members returning Streams and Sinks. The key parameter of `Context.make`, `add`, `get`, `pick`, `omit`, `Layer.succeed`, `Layer.effect`, `Layer.mock`, `Effect.service`, `Effect.provideService`, `Effect.updateService` and their `Stream`, `Sink` and `Channel` counterparts widens from `Context.Key` to the new supertype `Context.Slot`; `Context.Key` is unchanged.
