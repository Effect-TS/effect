Add the `@effect/graphql-generator` package: the GraphQL language front end (lexer, parser, compact executable-document printer and located diagnostics) that the generator for `effect/graphql` builds on, and the `graphqlgen` CLI, which loads `graphql.config.ts` and writes, checks (`--check`), watches (`--watch`) and cleans up the generated `.graphql.ts` modules.
